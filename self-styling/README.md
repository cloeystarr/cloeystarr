# Self-styling with an AI agent

By [Chloe Starr](https://chloestarr.me).

This is the styling brief I developed for myself. I use it with my agents to get a yes-or-no judgment on individual items of clothing I’m considering buying online.

It brings together my style identity, body and fit considerations, silhouettes, necklines, colors, fabrics, outfit formulas and shopping filters. It is a record of my own preferences, rather than a set of rules everyone should dress by.

## The brief

[Read or copy my self-styling brief](styling-brief.md).

Personal measurements and clothing sizes have been removed from this public version. The rest preserves the archived brief. My wardrobe and preferences can change over time.

## How I use it

Give an agent the brief alongside a specific item: its link, photographs, fabric, price, size chart and garment measurements when available. Ask:

> Using my styling brief, would you say yes or no to this item? Explain which fit, neckline, silhouette, color and fabric details drive your decision. If the listing leaves something important unclear, tell me what information is missing rather than guessing. Does it solve a wardrobe gap and work with clothes I already own?

If you adapt it, replace my preferences with your own and supply any personal fit information privately. A recommendation is a decision aid; it does not authorize an agent to buy anything.
