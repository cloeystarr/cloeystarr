# Claude Styling Brief for Chloe

## Quick Snapshot
- **Core style identity:** Theatrical Romantic. Feminine, sophisticated, romantic, softly glamorous. Think vintage screen siren meets modern soft power.
- **Bust note:** Prioritize necklines and cuts that frame the bust and décolletage without flattening or over compressing it.
- **Fit priorities:** Waist emphasis, body skimming fabrics, drape over stiffness, soft curve accommodation, elegant long sleeves.
- **Color direction:** Jewel tones first, especially emerald. Also sapphire, amethyst, ruby, deep teal. Romantic neutrals like cream, taupe, black can work when the silhouette is dramatic or sensual.
- **Current shopping focus:** Fitted long sleeve sweaters and cardigans, jewel toned wrap tops, bias cut skirts, flattering curvy pants, draped light layers and pashminas.

## Silhouette Rules
- Always favor pieces that define the waist: wrap cuts, belts, princess seams, shaped knits, darted waists, peplum details, fitted bodices.
- Best overall lines are curve conscious rather than tight everywhere. Aim for skim, drape, and shape.
- Bias cut skirts and dresses are better than stiff A line shapes if both are equally practical.
- Nothing boxy, oversized, straight up and down, or heavily dropped shoulder unless it can be styled back into visible waist definition.
- Avoid rigid workwear energy unless the piece is still distinctly feminine and TR aligned.

## Bust and Neckline Guidance
- Best necklines: scoop, deep V, sweetheart, soft square, surplice, off shoulder, open wrap fronts.
- Use necklines that break up the chest visually and highlight the décolletage.
- Look for adjustable wrap ties, stretch panels, darts, smocking at the back, or well placed seaming so the bust fits without the waist going shapeless.
- Avoid high crew necks, mock necks, severe button ups, and flat unshaped fronts.
- For knits, fitted rib is fine only when it looks elegant and not sporty or cheap.

## Best Categories
- Wrap tops in jewel tones or luxe matte jerseys, silks, satins, fine knits, or draped viscose blends.
- Long sleeve cardigans and sweaters that are fitted, cropped to the high hip, belted, peplum, or softly draped.
- Bias cut skirts in satin or silk, especially emerald or neutral shades that mix across outfits.
- Dresses with fitted bodices and flowing skirts, especially wrap, bias, or vintage inspired silhouettes.
- Pants should be mid to high rise with curvy fit logic, waist shaping, stretch, darts, or tailoring.

## Hard No List
- No crop tops.
- No bodysuits.
- No sequins.
- No boxy knits, oversized silhouettes, or boxy tees.
- No high necklines that obscure the bust line.
- No rigid fabrics without waist shaping.
- No fast fashion style recommendations like Fashion Nova.
- Do not recommend Free People, Quince, Anine Bing, Banana Republic type picks, or brands with a boho, oversized, or generic corporate energy unless a very specific item clearly breaks the pattern.

## Color and Fabric Guidance
- Emerald is the top priority color.
- Other strong options: sapphire, amethyst, ruby, deep teal, black, cream, taupe.
- Skip dull gray and harsh geometrics.
- Natural and quality feeling fabrics are preferred.
- Machine washable is a plus, but not at the expense of silhouette.

## Shopping Filters
- Prioritize sales and Poshmark.
- New with tags only whenever possible.
- Favor brands that feel elevated, feminine, and resale worthy.
- Prioritize pieces that mix easily into repeat outfits.
- For pants, default search terms should include curvy, high rise, darted waist, stretch, tailored waist, or adjustable waist.
- For sweaters, prioritize long sleeve fitted pullovers and cardigans only.

## Best Outfit Formulas
- Wrap top + curvy high rise pant + soft boot or elegant flat + cardigan or pashmina.
- Fitted sweater + bias cut skirt + sculptural flat or heeled boot + soft jewelry.
- Waist defined dress + romantic layer + comfortable polished shoe.
- Scoop neck base top + draped cardigan + shaped trouser + feminine boot.
- Even casual pieces should still show shape and softness rather than reading sloppy.

## Brand and Style Direction
- General inspiration: Lana Del Rey energy, vintage screen siren, romantic glamour, modern soft power.
- User likes wrap tops and responds well to that category.
- Well known, resale worthy, more feminine brands are better than random unbranded marketplace finds.
- The goal is not minimalist basics. The goal is beautiful everyday pieces that make her feel like herself.

## Final Decision Rule
When choosing between two items, pick the one that defines the waist, accommodates my bust, uses a romantic neckline, feels high quality, works with multiple outfits, and avoids boxiness. Do not recommend something just because it is on sale. It still has to be TR aligned and solve a wardrobe gap.
